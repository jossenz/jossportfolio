About

Hi, I'm Jossen, a healthcare background graphic designer specializing in User Experience

This very different background has led me to approach problems from another perspective, adopt a scientific mindset for research and develop genuine empathy — and a genuine passion for the intersection of technology, health and design. I focus on user experience because I enjoy understanding how things work beneath the surface: the research, the grounded decisions, the how and the why.

Visual identity and poster design form that other part of me. I enjoy the process from conceptualization to execution, and bringing something visible everywhere. I'm passionate about art and culture — that's where I find my spark of inspiration!

Feel free to contact me for projects!
