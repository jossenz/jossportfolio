How do you translate decades of history in live sound into a visual identity as technical and approachable as the company it represents?

Nombre del proyecto

Client
Audiosistemas C.A.
Industry
Audiovisual entertainment
Location
Mérida, Venezuela
Year
2026
Tools

Corporate identity and wayfinding.

Audiosistemas is a Venezuelan audiovisual production company specialising in live sound, lighting, LED screens and event staging, with nearly five decades of history in the entertainment industry. The project involved designing a complete brand identity system from scratch — covering the full discovery process, visual identity creation, and a wayfinding system for the company's central warehouse.

The identity is built around an abstract microphone symbol constructed on an isometric grid, paired with a custom wordmark set in Eco typeface. A strict black and white palette reflects the nature of the business — live events, stages, and nighttime productions — with two optional accent colours defined for digital and print contexts respectively.

As part of the project, a family of eleven geometric icons was designed for the warehouse's internal signage system, alongside a complete set of regulatory and orientational signs, technical specifications for three different sign support types, and a full implantation plan for the space.

The result is a deployable brand manual — designed not only as an academic deliverable but as a real, functional identity system ready for implementation.
