Jossen Zambrano

Designer across media — visual identity, UX, and everything in between.

I approach design the way I once approached clinical problems — with curiosity, structure, and more questions than answers at first. That mindset is still here. It just found a better medium.

I work across visual identity and UX/UI because I genuinely don't think those two things should live apart. A brand that doesn't work digitally isn't really a brand. An app that doesn't feel like something isn't really an experience.

I believe in learning out loud. This portfolio is not a greatest hits, but an honest record of where I am and where I'm going. Every project here is a step, not exactly a destination.

Based in Madrid. Open to the world.
