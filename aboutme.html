About

Based in Madrid, specializing in visual identity and UX/UI design.

Trained in graphic design, with a different background that has led me to approach problems from another perspective, adopt a scientific mindset for research and develop genuine empathy. I focus on user experience because I enjoy understanding how things work beneath the surface: the research, the grounded decisions, the how and the why.

Visual identity and poster design form that other part of me. I enjoy the process from conceptualization to execution, and bringing something visible everywhere. I'm passionate about art and culture — that's where I find my spark of inspiration!.

Feel free to contact me for fun projects!
